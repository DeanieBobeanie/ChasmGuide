ChasmGuide 1.3.0-beta.2
21 October 2026

This release includes the README and screenshot update contributed by Hacker--x in PR #1. The repository now has clearer installation and usage instructions, cover artwork and three additional in-game screenshots. Gameplay behavior, quest data, saved progress and settings are unchanged from 1.3.0-beta.1.

Install ChasmGuide-1.3.0-beta.2.zip into Interface/AddOns, keeping your WTF folder. Restart WoW and open the guide with /cguide. Existing beta 1 users do not need to update for gameplay fixes.

This remains a beta for WoW Forever 1.60.1, interface 16001. Five catalog entries have no verified playable route. Full dungeon runs and live quest interactions still need in-game checks.

Earlier route notes follow.

ChasmGuide 1.3.0 and ChasmGuideRXP 1.1.0
Reviewed 17 October 2026

Changes

City of Dalaran now has four dungeon quests for each faction. The route collects outside pickups before entry, includes Horde key preparation, and returns each Heart of Disruption variant to the correct NPC. Alliance also gets the optional Southshore breadcrumb and Friend of the Kirin Tor follow-up. Outdoor NPC pins come from QuestieDB 1.0.6. Interior steps use quest objectives and written directions.

Past Due is now in the Alliance Scarlet Monastery checklist. It starts at level 30 with Magus Olvek outside Dalaran. Recover The Lost Magic of the Runemasters in the Library and return to him.

Excavation Site uses Blizzard's level range of 26-31. Razorfen Downs and Uldaman explain their beta access levels. Access levels do not guarantee that a group can clear the dungeon.

Blizzard opened Dalaran on 8 October. The 15 October notes do not announce another dungeon opening. Five announced dungeons still have no verified route here: The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's Terrace.

Blizzard also confirmed that Horde are attacked on sight in outdoor Dalaran, even after the key chain. Dungeon access is unaffected. Shrewd Negotiations stays as a blocked reference with no city waypoint or automatic quest step. Older articles describing unrestricted Horde city access are outdated.

Upgrade

Fully exit and restart WoW. The update adds files that a reload may not discover.

ChasmGuide retains quest cursors and skips by quest ID for the changed routes. Finished routes stay finished. Use /cguide restart to review additions. Unmatched manual checkpoints return to the first step. The addon retains the old cursor and skips in its own saved data.

In RestedXP, choose City of Dalaran [October update] or Scarlet Monastery [October update] under WoW Forever Dungeons. These changed routes have new names to prevent reuse of old step numbers. They begin at the first checkpoint and read completed quests from the game. Other route names remain unchanged.

Arrow and automation switches still default to off. Existing settings are preserved. Reward choices stay manual. RestedXP's installed files and SavedVariables were not edited.

Checks and limits

The original addon regression tests passed. New tests passed 672 migration cases, including active and inactive saves, skipped steps, completed routes and faction switches. The installed RestedXP v4.11.22 parser passed all 448 dungeon, faction, class and settings combinations. Both factions register 28 guides each.

This review checked dungeon availability and newly added or changed quest records against Blizzard's notes, current Forever quest pages, and the installed QuestieDB 1.0.6 snapshot. It confirmed that the Deadmines explosives chain, A Frightened Request, and the Hall of Thanes map prerequisite were already covered. Conflicting database-only changes were not used to replace confirmed quest text.

This is not a fresh in-game test of every dungeon or a revalidation of every Classic quest. Interior coordinates, current NPC interactions, and the final gameplay flow still need in-game testing. Five future dungeon routes remain unverified.
