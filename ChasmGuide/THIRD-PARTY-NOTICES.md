## Quest metadata

CatalogData.lua contains a factual quest and location snapshot derived from the installed [QuestieDB](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB) on 4 October 2026. Later route corrections used the installed QuestieDB 1.0.6 snapshot and the sources listed in README.md and RELEASE-NOTES.txt.

The QuestieDB maintainers permit forks and copying under GPLv3 while noting that some historic contributions have unresolved licensing. Their complete notice, retrieved on 21 October 2026, is preserved in QUESTIEDB-LICENSE.md. This package does not claim that every historic contributor has granted a new license.

WowheadData.lua stores factual quest metadata and source links. Source articles and web HTML are not bundled. Quest names, game text and World of Warcraft belong to their respective rights holders. The project's code license does not claim ownership of those materials.

The 1.2.1 files under tests/fixtures preserve earlier ChasmGuide behavior for migration checks and share these source notices. They are not part of the installable ZIP.
